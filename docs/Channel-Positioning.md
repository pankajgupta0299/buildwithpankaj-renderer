## Core identity

- **Brand and Instagram handle:** BuildWithPankaj · `@buildwith_pankaj`
- **Mission:** Teach anyone to build useful, creative, or fun things with AI through clear, practical examples.
- **Positioning:** A place to learn by building with AI. Projects may be apps, games, websites, automations, visual experiments, content, personal tools, or ideas we have not tried yet.
- **Audience:** Anyone curious about making something with AI, from complete beginners to experienced builders. Explain concepts without assuming a job title, industry, coding experience, or paid tool access; give advanced viewers a useful next step when relevant.
- **Promise:** Show what to build, why it matters, and the steps someone can try. Demonstrate real outcomes, label illustrated examples, and share limitations honestly.
- **Tagline:** Build anything with AI. Learn by doing.

## Content direction

1. **Build it:** An actionable project or small feature with visible progress and a working outcome.
2. **Make it yours:** Show how to change a prompt, design, dataset, or rule for a different use.
3. **Learn the method:** Teach a transferable skill such as planning, prompting, debugging, testing, or publishing.
4. **Experiment honestly:** Share useful tools and emerging techniques with verifiable claims and clear limits.

Every Reel opens with the thing the audience will learn or build, then shows enough of the actual process to reproduce the essential step. Examples can serve work, daily life, hobbies, learning, or entertainment. Avoid treating professional outcomes as the channel's default. Keep the approved visual system (olive, white, charcoal), direct teaching voice, trust rules, and exact asset approval process.
